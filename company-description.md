## Company Mission & Philosophy

Local Logic IT is a **local technology partner** dedicated to providing straightforward, honest IT support for both homes and businesses in the St. Louis region.

### Core Beliefs
- **Technology should work for you, not the other way around**
- No call centers, no confusing jargon—just real people solving real problems
- Local accountability matters: direct contact, named technicians, fast response times
- Practical solutions over flashy complexity

### Brand Promise
We deliver **local service with logical solutions**. Our team is based here, knows your business or home personally, and acts with real ownership over your technology challenges.

## **"The Logic Fix" — Business Onboarding Process**

Our branded approach to transforming business IT:

### Step 1: **The Logic Audit**
Deep-dive security and performance audit of current infrastructure. Identify "low hanging fruit" for immediate improvement.

### Step 2: **Standardization**
Unify your tech stack (M365 or Google Workspace). Implement centralized device management.

### Step 3: **Security Hardening**
Deploy Zero Trust security posture, passkeys, advanced DNS filtering, and automated backup testing.

### Step 4: **AI Integration**
Identify repetitive workflows and introduce local AI automation tools that save staff hours.

### Step 5: **Ongoing Logic**
24/7 system monitoring. Local team handles problems before staff realizes they exist.

### Step 6: **Quarterly Strategy**
Every 90 days: review tech roadmap and ensure technology scales alongside your business.
