How It Works

From risk review to practical protection

  1. Assess

    We review accounts, devices, backups, email settings, remote access, and the policies staff actually follow. You get a risk report showing exactly where your business is exposed — prioritized by severity and ease of exploitation.

  2. Harden

    MFA, patching, endpoint protection, least-privilege access, and backup controls are tightened first. We focus on the changes that eliminate the most risk with the least disruption to your daily operations.

  3. Train

    Staff get plain-English guidance on phishing, password habits, payment change requests, and suspicious prompts. We run simulated phishing tests to measure improvement and identify who needs extra support.

  4. Detect & Respond

    Security alerts are monitored for early signals that an account or device needs attention. If something looks wrong, we contain the issue, preserve evidence, and help the business recover cleanly and quickly.

Real-World Examples

How we've helped real businesses stay safe

CEO Email Impersonation Stopped

An attacker spoofed the CEO's email address and sent a wire transfer request to the accounting department. Our email security flagged the impersonation attempt, quarantined the message, and alerted the real CEO. The $47,000 fraudulent transfer was never processed.

$47,000 fraudulent wire transfer prevented

Ransomware Blocked at the Door

An employee clicked a link in a convincing invoice email. Our endpoint protection detected the ransomware payload within 3 seconds, isolated the workstation, and prevented any file encryption. We re-imaged the machine and traced the attack vector to improve email filtering rules.

Ransomware contained in 3 seconds — zero files encrypted

Phishing Click Rate Dropped 85%

A 30-person law firm had a 40% click rate on our initial phishing simulation — nearly half the staff clicked fake malicious links. After 6 months of monthly training and simulated campaigns, their click rate dropped to 6%. Three employees now regularly report suspicious emails to IT before opening them.

Phishing susceptibility dropped from 40% to 6%

200 Reused Passwords Found & Fixed

A security audit of a 25-person company revealed 200+ instances of password reuse across business accounts — including the same password used for the domain admin and a personal Netflix account. We deployed a password manager company-wide, enforced unique passwords, and enabled MFA on all critical systems.

200 reused passwords replaced — MFA on all critical accounts
