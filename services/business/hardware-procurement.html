How It Works

From spec confusion to ready-to-work equipment

  1. Consult

    We discuss what each role needs — an accountant running QuickBooks has different requirements than a graphic designer or a front desk receptionist. We spec equipment based on actual workload, not marketing hype.

  2. Source

    We source business-grade equipment from authorized distributors at competitive pricing. You get the same brands enterprise companies use — Dell, Lenovo, HP — at prices that make sense for your business size.

  3. Configure

    Every machine is unpacked, updated, configured with your software and security tools, joined to your network, and tested before delivery. Your team never has to sit through a 2-hour setup process.

  4. Deploy

    We deliver and set up equipment at your office or ship directly to remote workers. Old equipment is securely wiped and recycled. We handle the full lifecycle from unboxing to disposal.

Real-World Examples

How we've helped real businesses equip their teams

12 Laptops Deployed in a Day

A startup hired 12 employees and needed everyone productive on day one. We sourced 12 business laptops, pre-loaded them with Microsoft 365, VPN, and company security tools, and delivered them to the office the night before. Every employee opened their laptop Monday morning and was working within 5 minutes.

12 employees productive within 5 minutes of arrival

Spec'd Right, Not Over-Spec'd

A design firm was about to spend $2,800 per machine on top-tier workstations for their entire team. After reviewing actual workloads, we found that only 3 designers needed high-end specs. The other 12 employees worked fine on $1,100 business laptops. We saved them over $20,000 without sacrificing performance where it mattered.

$20,000 saved by right-sizing specs to actual workloads

Planned Replacement Prevented Downtime

Our hardware inventory tracking flagged that 8 machines in a client's office were approaching 5 years old with expired warranties. We planned a phased replacement over 3 months, migrating users one at a time during low-activity periods. Zero unplanned downtime from aging hardware failures.

8 machines replaced over 3 months — zero unplanned downtime

Secure Disposal of 30 Old Machines

A financial services firm needed to retire 30 computers containing client data. We performed certified data wiping on every drive, provided destruction certificates for compliance records, and coordinated responsible e-waste recycling. The client had auditable proof that no data left those machines.

30 machines wiped with certificates — full compliance documentation
