How It Works

From old phone lines to modern communication

  1. Assess

    We review your current phone usage — call volume, number of lines, remote workers, and must-have features. We audit your internet connection to ensure it can handle VoIP traffic without quality issues.

  2. Design

    We design a phone system that matches how your business actually communicates — including call routing, business hours, after-hours handling, and department ring groups. You review and approve the design before we touch anything.

  3. Deploy

    We provision desk phones, configure the cloud PBX, set up voicemail boxes, port your numbers, and test every extension. The cutover is scheduled at a time that won't disrupt your business operations.

  4. Train

    We walk each team through their new phones — transferring calls, setting up voicemail, using the mobile app, and managing their availability status. You get quick-reference guides for common tasks.

Real-World Examples

How we've helped real businesses upgrade their phones

$800/Month Phone Bill Cut in Half

A 25-person real estate office was paying $800/month for traditional phone lines plus a separate fax service. We migrated them to a cloud VoIP system with virtual fax. Their new monthly cost is $375 — a savings of $5,100 per year with better features including mobile apps and call recording.

$5,100 annual savings with better features than before

Remote Team Stays Connected

A consulting firm with 10 remote employees was using personal cell phones for business calls — no shared directory, no call transfer, and clients calling personal numbers. We deployed a cloud phone system with softphone apps. Now everyone has a business number, can transfer calls, and the company auto-attendant routes calls professionally.

Professional phone presence for fully remote team

After-Hours Calls Handled Automatically

A medical supply company was missing after-hours calls that went to a generic voicemail. We programmed an auto attendant with business hours routing, an after-hours menu for urgent orders, and automatic text notifications to the on-call manager. Order inquiries now get handled 24/7.

After-hours orders captured — no more missed revenue

3 Offices, One Phone System

A business with 3 locations had separate phone systems at each — no unified directory, no cross-location transfers, and inconsistent voicemail. We consolidated everything into one cloud system. Employees at any location can now transfer calls, see each other's availability, and share a single auto attendant.

3 locations unified — seamless calling across all offices
