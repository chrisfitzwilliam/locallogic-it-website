How It Works

From uncertainty to a clear technology plan

  1. Discover

    We spend time understanding your business — not just your technology. What are your goals, pain points, growth plans, and budget constraints? The best IT strategy starts with understanding the business it serves.

  2. Assess

    We audit your current IT environment top to bottom — documenting every system, identifying risks, and measuring how well your technology supports your daily operations. You get a clear, honest picture of where things stand.

  3. Recommend

    We present a prioritized roadmap of improvements — each one tied to a specific business outcome, estimated cost, and timeline. You decide which projects to greenlight based on your priorities and budget.

  4. Execute

    We can implement the recommendations ourselves or work alongside your existing team or vendors. Either way, we stay involved to ensure projects deliver the outcomes we promised in the plan.

Real-World Examples

How we've helped real businesses make smarter IT decisions

Saved $35K on Software Selection

A growing logistics company was about to sign a $50K/year contract for an enterprise ERP system they didn't need. After assessing their actual requirements, we recommended a mid-market solution at $15K/year that covered 100% of their needs. The selection process included demos, reference checks, and a trial period before commitment.

$35,000/year saved — better-fit software selected

IT Roadmap for a 5-Location Expansion

A dental practice chain planning to grow from 2 to 5 locations needed their IT to scale. We designed a standardized technology stack — cloud-based practice management, VoIP, and centralized security management — that could be replicated at each new location in days instead of weeks.

New locations deployable in 3 days instead of 3 weeks

Compliance Gap Identified Before Audit

A financial advisory firm was preparing for a regulatory compliance audit. Our assessment uncovered 14 security gaps — including unencrypted client data, no backup verification, and shared admin passwords. We remediated every issue before the auditor arrived. The firm passed with zero findings.

14 compliance gaps fixed — audit passed with zero findings

Smooth Technology Transition for Acquisition

When a 30-person company acquired a 15-person competitor, they had two separate email systems, file servers, and phone systems. We planned and executed the technology merger — migrating email, consolidating file storage, unifying the phone system, and standardizing security. All 45 employees were on one platform within 6 weeks.

Two companies merged onto one IT platform in 6 weeks
