How It Works

From on-premise to cloud — with zero disruption

  1. Assess

    We audit your current email, files, and applications to understand what's moving, what stays, and what needs special handling. We identify potential issues before the migration starts — not during it.

  2. Plan

    You get a detailed migration plan with timelines, user communication templates, and rollback procedures. We schedule the migration around your business hours so productivity isn't affected.

  3. Migrate

    We execute the migration in phases — testing with a pilot group first, then rolling out to the full team. Email, files, calendars, and contacts are verified at every step to ensure nothing is lost.

  4. Support

    After migration, we provide hands-on training and dedicated support as your team adjusts. We monitor for issues, answer questions, and optimize settings based on how people actually work.

Real-World Examples

How we've helped real businesses move to the cloud

50 Mailboxes Migrated Over a Weekend

A 50-person company needed to move from an aging on-premise Exchange server to Microsoft 365. We migrated all mailboxes, calendars, and contacts over a weekend. Monday morning, employees logged in to find all their email exactly where they left it — most didn't realize anything had changed.

50 mailboxes migrated — zero business disruption

10 Years of Files to SharePoint

A law firm had 10 years of client files on a local server with no remote access. We migrated 2TB of organized files to SharePoint with proper permissions, trained staff on the new system, and set up automatic sync so files are available on laptops and phones. The old server was decommissioned.

2TB migrated — remote file access for the first time

$9K Saved on Unused Licenses

A growing company had been adding Microsoft 365 licenses for 3 years without ever removing departed employees. We found 22 active licenses for people who no longer worked there and 8 users on premium plans who only needed basic email. The cleanup saved $9,000 annually.

$9,000 annual savings from license right-sizing

Ransomware Recovery from Cloud Backup

An employee clicked a phishing link that encrypted their OneDrive files. Because we had cloud backup in place, we restored the entire account to a point-in-time snapshot from 2 hours before the attack. No data was lost and the user was back to work within 30 minutes.

Full recovery in 30 minutes — zero data lost to ransomware
