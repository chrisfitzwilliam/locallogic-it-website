How receipt automation works with QuickBooks

Our Build Pattern

Five stages, every automation

Whether it's a five-step receipt processor or a thirty-step customer onboarding flow, every automation we build follows the same five stages so that nothing ever runs in a black box.

  1. Capture

    Forms, emails, invoices, lead notes, and documents are routed into one clear intake process.

  2. Extract

    AI reads the useful fields, summarizes context, and flags anything that needs a human decision.

  3. Decide

    Rules and approvals keep the workflow grounded so automation supports judgment instead of replacing it.

  4. Push

    Approved data moves into the tools your team already uses — CRM, accounting, email, ticketing.

  5. Audit

    Every handoff leaves a trail so leaders can review outcomes, errors, and time saved without guesswork.
