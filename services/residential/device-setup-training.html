What We Do

What's Included

  • Computer & Laptop Setup

    We unbox, configure, update, and personalize your new Windows PC or Mac so it's ready to use — not half-configured out of the box.

    • Windows/macOS initial setup and updates
    • User account creation and personalization
    • Essential software installation (browser, email, Office)

  • Data Transfer & Migration

    Switching devices shouldn't mean losing your files, photos, bookmarks, or email. We move everything over cleanly and verify nothing is missing.

    • Files, photos, and documents transferred
    • Email account setup and bookmark migration

  • Phone, Tablet & Smart Device Setup

    • Tablet and phone initial configuration
    • Smart TV and streaming device setup

    We sit with you and walk through everything you need to know — email, web browsing, video calls, photos, and whatever else matters to you.

    • Email, browsing, and video calling basics
    • Photo management and cloud backup
    • Security habits: passwords, scams, updates
How It Works

From unboxing to comfortable and confident

  1. Unbox

    We unpack your new device, check everything's included, and handle the tedious initial setup — creating accounts, running updates, and getting through all the 'first-time' prompts that eat up an afternoon.

  2. Transfer

    We move your files, photos, email accounts, bookmarks, and saved passwords from your old device to the new one. We verify everything transferred correctly before touching the old machine.

  3. Configure

    We install the apps you use, set up your printer, configure your email, connect to Wi-Fi, and adjust settings to your preferences — font sizes, display brightness, notification preferences, and more.

  4. Train

    We sit with you and walk through everything at your pace. How to send email, join a video call, manage photos, use your apps — plus tips on staying safe from scams and keeping your device running well.

Real-World Examples

Stories from real setups we've done

First Computer at 72

A grandmother received a laptop as a gift but had never used a computer. Over two visits, we set up email, taught her to video call her grandchildren on Zoom, showed her how to browse recipes and watch YouTube, and set up her phone to sync photos. She now emails and video calls independently every week.

From never touching a computer to weekly video calls

PC to Mac Transition

A professional switching from a 10-year-old Windows PC to a new MacBook was worried about losing years of documents. We transferred all files, set up her familiar apps (Word, Excel, Chrome), migrated her email, and spent an hour walking through the differences between Windows and Mac. She was productive the next morning.

Zero downtime — working on the new Mac the next day

iPad Setup for a Retiree

A retired teacher wanted an iPad for reading, email, and keeping up with family on Facebook. We set up his Apple ID, configured email, installed his reading apps, connected to his home Wi-Fi, and showed him how to FaceTime. We also increased the text size and set up Do Not Disturb for nighttime.

Reading books and FaceTiming family within the first week

Smart Home Entertainment Overhaul

A family had a new smart TV, a Roku stick, and a soundbar — all still in boxes. We mounted nothing but connected everything: TV to Wi-Fi, Roku configured with their Netflix/Hulu/Disney+ accounts, soundbar paired via HDMI ARC, and universal remote programmed. One remote now controls everything.

All devices working with one remote — no more input switching
