How It Works

From slow or stuck to working again

  1. Diagnose

    We run a full check on startup behavior, storage health, memory, system updates, error logs, and the day-to-day symptoms you've been dealing with. No guessing — we trace the actual root cause before touching anything.

  2. Quote

    You'll get a plain-English explanation of what we found, what's worth fixing, and what might not justify the cost. We believe in honest assessments — if a new machine makes more sense, we'll tell you that too.

  3. Repair

    Whether it's cleaning up software issues, tuning Windows settings, replacing a failing drive, or installing more RAM — we handle it on-site. Most repairs are done in a single visit without needing to take your computer away.

  4. Verify

    Before we leave, we test the system with you to make sure everything feels right. We'll walk you through what we changed, what to keep an eye on, and simple habits that keep your PC running smoothly long-term.

Real-World Examples

Stories from real repairs we've done

The Laptop That Kept Shutting Down

A family's 4-year-old HP laptop would randomly power off during video calls and homework. The previous repair shop said the motherboard was dying. We opened it up and found the cooling fan was completely clogged with dust and the thermal paste had dried out. After a thorough cleaning and thermal paste replacement, the laptop ran cool and stable — saving the family the cost of a new machine.

Fixed for under $100 — still running a year later

The Desktop That Took 12 Minutes to Boot

A home office desktop running Windows 10 was taking over 12 minutes from power button to usable. The owner had tried "cleaning" programs that made things worse. We found 47 startup programs, a nearly full hard drive, and years of accumulated temp files. After a cleanup, startup optimization, and an SSD upgrade, boot time dropped to under 30 seconds.

Boot time went from 12 minutes to 28 seconds

Blue Screen Errors Every Afternoon

A college student's gaming PC would blue-screen reliably between 2–5 PM every day. Standard diagnostics showed nothing obvious. We traced it to a failing RAM stick that only caused errors under heavy load when the room temperature rose in the afternoon sun. Replacing the faulty memory module solved the issue completely — no more lost homework or crashed gaming sessions.

One $45 RAM stick fixed months of frustration

Clicking Hard Drive, Precious Photos

A grandparent's desktop started making clicking sounds and running extremely slowly. They had 15 years of family photos, tax documents, and letters stored on a single hard drive with no backup. We carefully cloned the failing drive to a new SSD before it died completely, set up automatic cloud backup for the future, and gave them a USB backup copy for extra peace of mind.

15 years of memories saved — now backed up in 3 places
