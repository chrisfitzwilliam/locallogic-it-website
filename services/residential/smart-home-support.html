How It Works

From scattered gadgets to a connected home

  1. Plan

    We discuss what you want to automate, which devices you already own, and what ecosystem (Alexa, Google, Apple) works best for your household. We create a practical plan that avoids compatibility headaches.

  2. Install

    We unbox, position, mount, and connect every device — from smart bulbs and cameras to thermostats and sensors. Everything gets properly connected to your Wi-Fi and registered to your accounts.

  3. Integrate

    We link everything together through your voice assistant or hub. Scenes, routines, and automations get programmed — like 'goodnight' turning off all lights, locking the door, and arming cameras.

  4. Train

    We walk you through controlling everything — voice commands, phone apps, physical switches, and what to do if something stops responding. You'll feel confident managing your smart home independently.

Real-World Examples

Stories from real smart homes we've set up

Whole-House Smart Lighting

A couple wanted smart lighting throughout their home but had a mix of Hue, LIFX, and generic Wi-Fi bulbs. We consolidated everything under one ecosystem, set up room groups, and created scenes for morning, dinner, movie night, and bedtime. One voice command now controls every light in the house.

One voice command controls 22 smart lights across 8 rooms

Complete Security Camera System

A homeowner wanted cameras covering the front door, garage, and backyard but didn't want monthly cloud fees. We installed 4 cameras with local NVR recording, configured motion zones to avoid false alerts from passing cars, and set up phone notifications for person detection only.

24/7 recording with zero monthly fees and smart alerts

Smart Thermostat Saving $40/Month

A family was heating and cooling an empty house 10 hours a day. We installed an Ecobee thermostat with room sensors, programmed an occupancy-based schedule, and enabled geofencing so it adjusts when everyone leaves. Their energy bill dropped $40/month within the first billing cycle.

$480/year savings with automated temperature scheduling

Alexa Running the Whole House

A tech-curious family had Alexa devices in 4 rooms but only used them for weather and timers. We connected their Ring doorbell, smart plugs, robot vacuum, and garage door opener. Now they have 'leaving home' and 'arriving home' routines that handle locks, lights, thermostat, and cameras automatically.

Complete home automation with voice and one-tap routines
