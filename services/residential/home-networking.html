Reliable Home Networking

Dead zones, slow Wi-Fi, and devices that won't connect are frustrating. We design, install, and optimize home networks so every room gets strong, stable coverage.

Fix My Wi-Fi
What We Do

What's Included

  • Wi-Fi Assessment & Optimization

    We map your home's wireless coverage, identify dead zones and interference sources, then optimize placement and settings for maximum speed and reach.

    • Signal strength mapping room by room
    • Channel optimization to reduce neighbor interference
    • Band steering setup for 2.4 GHz vs 5 GHz devices

  • Wired Network Installation

    For home offices, gaming setups, and streaming rooms, nothing beats a wired connection. We run Ethernet cleanly and professionally.

    • Ethernet cable runs with wall plates
    • Switch installation for multi-device areas
    • Clean cable management and labeling

  • Router & Access Point Setup

    We configure your equipment properly — not just plug it in and hope. Mesh systems, access points, and range extenders placed where they actually help.

    • Mesh Wi-Fi system installation and configuration
    • Guest network setup for visitors
    • Quality of Service (QoS) for streaming and gaming

  • Network Security Hardening

    An unsecured home network is an open door. We lock it down with strong encryption, updated firmware, and proper device isolation.

    • WPA3 encryption and strong password setup
    • Router firmware updates and admin lockdown
    • IoT device isolation from personal computers
How It Works

From dead zones to whole-home coverage

  1. Survey

    We walk through your home testing signal strength in every room, identifying dead zones, interference sources, and the best locations for equipment. We also review your internet plan to make sure it matches your household's actual usage.

  2. Plan

    Based on the survey, we recommend the right equipment — whether that's repositioning your existing router, adding access points, or installing a mesh system. You get a clear plan with costs before we touch anything.

  3. Install

    We set up and configure all equipment, run any necessary cables, optimize wireless channels, and test every room for speed and reliability. Everything is labeled and documented for future reference.

  4. Verify

    Before we leave, we connect all your devices, run speed tests throughout the home, and walk you through the network — including your Wi-Fi password, guest network, and how to reboot if something acts up.

Real-World Examples

Stories from real installs we've done

The 3-Story Home With No Upstairs Wi-Fi

A family in a large colonial-style home had great Wi-Fi in the living room but nothing upstairs. Their ISP-provided router was in the basement. We installed a 3-node mesh system with one unit per floor, achieving 300+ Mbps on every level. The kids' homework sessions stopped buffering and the parents could finally work from the upstairs office.

Full coverage on all 3 floors — 300+ Mbps everywhere

The Gamer Who Kept Lagging

A teenager's gaming setup was on Wi-Fi across the house from the router, causing 80ms+ ping spikes during competitive matches. We ran a single Ethernet cable through the attic to his room with a clean wall plate. Ping dropped to 12ms and packet loss disappeared completely.

Ping dropped from 80ms to 12ms — zero packet loss

Security Cameras Crashing the Network

A homeowner installed 6 Wi-Fi security cameras and suddenly everything else slowed to a crawl. The cameras were consuming all the bandwidth on one channel. We put the cameras on a dedicated network segment with their own access point and configured QoS so streaming and work traffic stayed prioritized.

Cameras running 24/7 without affecting household internet

The Home Office That Dropped Calls

A remote worker's video calls would freeze and drop multiple times per day. Their Wi-Fi signal showed 'full bars' but was actually congested by 12 neighboring networks on the same channel. We switched to a less crowded 5GHz channel, repositioned the access point, and ran a short Ethernet cable to their desk as a backup. No dropped calls since.

Zero dropped calls in 6 months of daily video meetings
