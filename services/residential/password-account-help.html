Trusted Password & Account Help

Locked out of your email? Can't remember your Apple ID? Worried about getting hacked? We help you recover accounts, organize passwords, and secure your digital life.

Secure My Accounts
What We Do

What's Included

  • Account Recovery & Lockout Help

    Locked out of your email, Apple ID, Microsoft account, or social media? We walk through the recovery process with you and get you back in.

    • Email account recovery (Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo)
    • Apple ID and Microsoft account unlock
    • Social media account recovery and verification

  • Password Manager Setup

    Stop reusing passwords. We set up a password manager that securely stores all your logins and auto-fills them — so you only need to remember one master password.

    • Password manager installation and configuration
    • Importing existing passwords from browsers
    • Training on daily use and password generation

  • Two-Factor Authentication Setup

    Adding a second layer of security to your important accounts is the single best thing you can do to prevent hacking. We set it up on every account that supports it.

    • 2FA setup on email, banking, and social media
    • Authenticator app configuration
    • Backup codes saved in a secure location

  • Compromised Account Response

    If you've been hacked or think someone has access to your accounts, we act fast — changing passwords, revoking access, enabling security features, and checking for damage.

    • Immediate password changes on compromised accounts
    • Third-party app access revocation
    • Security review of connected devices and sessions
How It Works

From locked out to secure and organized

  1. Assess

    We review which accounts you're locked out of, which ones might be compromised, and what recovery options are available. We check breach databases to see if any of your passwords have been exposed in known data leaks.

  2. Recover

    We work through each account's recovery process — identity verification, recovery emails, phone verification, and support ticket escalation when needed. We've navigated every major provider's recovery flow.

  3. Secure

    Once accounts are recovered, we update passwords to strong unique values, enable two-factor authentication, revoke any unauthorized access, and set up a password manager to keep everything organized going forward.

  4. Educate

    We show you how to use your password manager, recognize phishing emails, and spot the warning signs that an account has been compromised — so you can protect yourself independently.

Real-World Examples

Stories from real recoveries we've done

Locked Out of Gmail for 3 Months

A senior lost access to her Gmail after getting a new phone and couldn't remember her password. The recovery phone number was her old number she no longer had. We navigated Google's account recovery process using her original account creation details and trusted device history to verify her identity and regain access.

Full Gmail access restored after 3 months of lockout

Hacked Facebook Spreading Scam Links

A man's Facebook account was hacked and was sending scam links to all his friends. The hacker had changed the email and password. We used Facebook's trusted contacts recovery, regained access, removed the attacker's email, changed the password, enabled 2FA, and revoked all active sessions on other devices.

Account recovered, secured with 2FA, scam posts removed

87 Passwords on Sticky Notes

A couple had 87 different account passwords written on sticky notes, backs of envelopes, and a Word document on the desktop. We set up a shared family password manager, entered all 87 accounts, generated new strong passwords for the most critical ones (banking, email), and taught them how to auto-fill on their phones and computer.

87 passwords organized — critical accounts now have unique, strong passwords

Apple ID Locked After Too Many Attempts

A teenager got locked out of her Apple ID after too many wrong password attempts, disabling her iPhone and iPad. We walked through Apple's account recovery process, verified her identity, reset the password, re-enabled both devices, and set up the family's iCloud Keychain for easier password management across all their Apple devices.

Both devices unlocked, Apple ID recovered, Keychain enabled
