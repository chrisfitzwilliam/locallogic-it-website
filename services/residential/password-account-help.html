Secure Passwords & Accounts

Locked out of an account or overwhelmed by passwords? We guide you through safe recovery steps and help you implement a logical, secure password manager.

Recover Your Account
What We Do

What's Included

  • Guidance through account recovery (Google, iCloud, Facebook, etc.)
  • Password manager (Bitwarden, 1Password, etc.) setup and training
  • Multi-factor authentication (MFA) setup for better security
  • Account security audits and privacy setting reviews
How It Works

From locked out to safer access

  1. Recover

    We walk through official recovery steps for email, device, banking, social, or shopping accounts.

  2. Secure

    Once access is restored, we review passwords, recovery email, phone numbers, and suspicious settings.

  3. Manage

    Password manager setup, passkeys, and safer sign-in habits are explained in a way that fits your comfort level.

  4. Save Codes

    Important recovery codes and account notes are stored safely so the next lockout is easier to handle.

Real-World Example

When one password blocks everything

A locked email account can quickly become a bigger problem because it controls password resets for banking, shopping, medical portals, and family photos. We help work through official recovery paths, then clean up the account so it is less fragile. That may include stronger passwords, MFA, updated recovery options, and printed backup instructions kept somewhere safe. The goal is secure access you can actually manage.
